Slide 3: Introduction to US Staffing
- What is US Staffing: Identifying, attracting, screening, and placing qualified US candidates.
- Objectives: Understand client requirements, source talent, screen resumes, and coordinate offers.
- Types of Hiring: Permanent, Contract, and Contract-to-Hire (C2H).
- Recruitment Flow: Requirement -> Sourcing -> Screening -> Submission -> Interview -> Offer -> Onboarding.

Slide 5: Market & Sourcing Landscape
- Major Sourcing Portals: LinkedIn, Dice, Indeed, Monster, CareerBuilder.
- Alternative Talent Pipelines: Employee Referrals, Internal ATS Database, GitHub.
- Key Sourcing Objectives: Build robust talent pipelines, reduce hiring time, and improve candidate quality.

Slide 6: US Time Zones & Logistics
- Eastern Time (ET): Financial services, legal sectors, and major corporate headquarters.
- Central Time (CT): Oil & gas, core manufacturing operations, and commercial logistics.
- Mountain Time (MT): Key tech hubs, engineering infrastructure, and aerospace corridors.
- Pacific Time (PT): Global technology development, software architecture, and biotech.
- Sourcing Impact: Critical for scheduling interviews, client meetings, and managing Daylight Saving Time.

Slide 7: Standard Corporate Hierarchy
- Executive Tier (T1-T2): Founders, Board Chairs, Presidents, CEOs, CTOs, CFOs (Strategic direction).
- Management Tier (T3-T4): Vice Presidents, Directors, Heads of Department, and Team Supervisors (Budgetary & delivery control).
- Operations Tier (T5): Individual Contributors, Clerical Teams, Staff, and Specialized Technicians (Execution).

Slide 8: US Tax & Payroll Systems
- Statutory Employee (W2): Candidate works directly as an employee. W2 company handles income tax withholding, FICA, and insurance.
- Independent Contractor (1099): Sole practitioners responsible for handling their own self-employment taxes.
- Corp-to-Corp (C2C): Business-to-business contract where payments are made directly to a candidate's corporate entity.

Slide 9: Candidate Search Strategy
- Sourcing Workflow: Receive JD -> Identify Keywords -> Construct Boolean Search -> Source Channels -> Shortlist -> Submit.
- Best Practices: Thoroughly understand the job description, utilize multiple unique platforms, and update internal ATS databases.

Slide 10: Mastering Boolean Search
- AND Operator: Binds keywords together ("AutoCAD" AND "Electrical").
- OR Operator: Expands streams with synonyms ("Designer" OR "Technician").
- NOT Operator: Restricts noise and filters out management profiles (Developer NOT "Manager").
- Modifiers: Quotation marks for exact strings ("Java Developer") and Parentheses for priority logic.
- Master Example: ("Java Developer" OR "Software Engineer") AND AWS NOT Intern.

Slide 11: Candidate Screening Framework
- Screening Process: Resume Review -> Skills Alignment -> Communication Check -> Work Authorization -> Salary/Availability.
- Core Intake Checklist: Confirm daily commute/location, establish compensation target, and certify visa validation.
- Technical Verification: Assess skills utilizing the 4-Tier Grid (Primary, Secondary, Desired, Preferred).

Slide 12: Candidate Engagement & Outreach
- Outreach Blueprint: Introduction -> Job Explanation -> Qualification Questions -> Interview Scheduling -> Offer Tracking.
- Standard Channels: Multi-channel engagement utilizing Email, Phone Calls, Voice Mail, and SMS.
- Cold-Calling Intro: "Hello, my name is Shaista Asmin. I'm calling regarding an exciting career opportunity matching your profile. Is this a good time to speak?"

Slide 13: Client Intake Rules
- Requirement Gathering: Job Title, Core Skills, Experience Range, Location, Target Budget, and Employment Type.
- Workflow Approvals: Client Requirement -> Team Discussion -> Detail Clarification -> Internal Approval -> Active Sourcing.

Slide 14: The Onboarding Pipeline
- Phase 1 (Sourced): Sourced via Boolean matrix arrays across competitor structures and passive databases.
- Phase 2 (Screened): Qualified through 4 channels (Email, Call, VM, SMS) using the Intake Checklist.
- Phase 3 (Submitted): Profile aligned to specific sectors and forwarded directly to the Account Manager.
- Phase 4 (Placed): End-client selection validation, final interviews completed, and offer locked on W2/C2C.

Slide 15: Recruitment Challenges & Solutions
- Core Obstacles: Candidate drops, unrealistic salary expectations, long notice periods, and steep skill gaps.
- Actionable Solutions: Maintain regular follow-ups, build strong candidate pipelines, and ensure accurate early screening.

Slide 16: Recruiter Best Practices
- Sourcing Guidelines: Read every JD carefully, source across diverse channels, and learn new technology terminologies.
- Relationship Capital: Maintain professional communication, practice strict recruiting ethics, and keep clean candidate data.
- "Great recruiters don't just fill positions—they build careers and lasting relationships."

Slide 17: Key Takeaways
- Always align technical needs with clear corporate tier and payroll structures (W2/C2C).
- Master Boolean logic and multi-channel communication to maximize submittal velocity.
- "The right candidate, for the right role, at the right time."
